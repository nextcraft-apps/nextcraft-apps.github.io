NX Player by NextCraft

Need help or found a bug? Write to nextcraft.support@gmail.com. Tell us your phone model and what happened; a screenshot helps.

Adding music

iPhone: put songs in the Files app (On My iPhone › NX Player), use Wi-Fi transfer from your computer, or share files to NX Player. You can also add any folder in Settings › Music folders.

Android: NX Player finds music in your Music and Download folders. Add other folders in Settings › Music folders.

Moving to a new phone

Settings › Backup › save a backup file, then open it on the new phone with Restore › Choose a backup file. Add your music folders again afterwards.

NX Player Pro

  • Restore a purchase: Settings › NX Player Pro › Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID or Google account.
  • Cancel a subscription: iPhone: Settings › your name › Subscriptions. Android: Google Play › Profile › Payments & subscriptions.
  • Refunds: Apple or Google Play.

Ads

The free version shows a few ads; Pro removes them. In Europe and the UK you can change your ad consent in Settings › Ad privacy choices.
