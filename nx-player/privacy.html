1. Who we are

NX Player is made by NextCraft Technologies, a software company in Nigeria ("NextCraft", "we", "us"). For the information described here that NextCraft decides how to use, NextCraft is the data controller. The services listed in section 4 are separate companies; each handles information under its own privacy policy, linked below.

Contact: nextcraft.support@gmail.com

2. Summary

  • No account. We never ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • Your music, playlists, likes, play counts, settings and backups stay on your device.
  • We don't run servers that collect your information, and we never upload your music, photos or files.
  • The free version shows ads. The ad company may use your device's advertising ID, IP address and approximate location. Where the law requires it, you are asked first, and you can change your choice any time. NX Player Pro has no ads.
  • Some features look things up online (lyrics, covers, song recognition, radio). Only what the feature needs is sent, such as a song's title and artist.
  • We don't sell your personal information.

3. Information that stays on your device

NX Player keeps the following only on your phone, in the app's own storage. We cannot see it.

  • Your music library: file names, folders, song details (tags), covers, lyrics you add.
  • Playlists, likes, play counts and history, ratings, tags, markers, queue.
  • Sound settings (equalizer, presets, headphone models), app settings and the folders you choose.
  • Recordings, ringtones, Status videos and mix files you create.
  • Backups you make. They are saved where you choose, for example in your phone's storage, Files or iCloud Drive.

Optional features that move this information between your own devices:

  • iCloud sync (iPhone): likes, plays and playlists are stored in your private iCloud space by Apple, under Apple's Privacy Policy. We cannot access it.
  • Wi-Fi transfer, phone sync and party mode: files and song requests go directly between devices on your own Wi-Fi network, not through us.
  • Casting (Chromecast): the song plays on your TV or speaker through your network, using Google Cast under Google's Privacy Policy.

4. Services NX Player uses

Like any internet request, each service below also receives your device's IP address, and usually basic technical details such as the app version, the operating system and the device type.

Google AdMob and Google's consent message (free version)

Receives: the advertising ID (IDFA on iPhone, only if you allow tracking; Android advertising ID), IP address and the approximate location derived from it, device and app information, ad views and taps, and your consent choice.

Why: to show ads, limit how often you see them, measure them and prevent fraud. It also shows the consent message where the law requires one.

Policy: Google Privacy Policy · How Google uses information in ads

Backup ad companies: AppLovin, Unity Ads, Start.io (free version)

If we switch the free version's ads to one of these companies, for example during an AdMob outage, that one company shows the ads instead of Google, using the same kinds of information, for the same purposes, and following the same consent choice. Only one ad company serves ads at a time.

Policies: AppLovin · Unity Ads · Start.io

Google Firebase (Remote Config, Cloud Messaging)

Receives: a Firebase installation ID, a notification token if you allow notifications, the app version and the device type.

Why: to change app settings without an update (for example, sales, which ad company is used, update reminders) and to deliver notifications you allowed.

Policy: Privacy and Security in Firebase

RevenueCat, App Store and Google Play (NX Player Pro)

Receives: RevenueCat receives a random app user ID that is not linked to your name, your purchase and subscription records (product, date, price, store country) and the app version. Payment is made through the App Store or Google Play; we never see your card or payment details.

Why: to sell Pro, check whether Pro is active and restore it on your devices.

Policies: RevenueCat · Apple · Google

Apple ShazamKit (Listen, naming songs in mixes)

Receives: an audio signature, a short digital fingerprint computed on your phone from a few seconds of sound. The recording itself is not sent and is not kept.

Why: to identify the song. Used only when you start Listen or ask NX Player to name the songs in a mix.

Policy: Apple Privacy Policy

Lyrics, song details, covers and radio

  • LRCLIB: the song's title, artist, album and length, to find lyrics.
  • MusicBrainz (MetaBrainz Foundation): the title, artist and album, to find song details and credits.
  • Apple iTunes Search: the title and artist, to find covers. Apple Privacy Policy.
  • Radio Browser: your station searches, to list radio stations. When you play a station, its stream comes directly from that station, which sees your IP address like any website you visit.

These lookups happen when you use those features, or when you turn on automatic lyrics or cover search.

5. What NextCraft receives

  • Reports from the services above: totals such as ad earnings and impressions, numbers of installs and notification deliveries, and purchase records linked only to random IDs. We do not receive your advertising ID, IP address or location from them in a form that identifies you.
  • Store reports from Apple and Google: downloads, crashes, ratings and reviews, as their consoles provide them.
  • Messages you send us: your email address and whatever you write, used only to answer you.

We don't combine this with other information to identify you, and we don't build profiles.

6. Advertising and your choices

  • Consent in Europe, the UK and Switzerland: before any ad, Google's message asks whether ads may be personalised. You can change your answer any time in NX Player under Settings › Ad privacy choices.
  • iPhone: Apple asks whether NX Player may track you. If you say no, the advertising ID is not shared and ads are not personalised with it. You can change it in iPhone Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
  • Android: you can delete or reset your advertising ID in Settings › Google › Ads (the wording varies by phone).
  • No ads at all: NX Player Pro removes all ads. A rewarded ad you choose to watch gives Pro for a short time.
  • Even without personalisation, ads still use information such as the IP address to show non-personalised ads, limit how often you see them and prevent fraud.

7. Permissions

NX Player asks only for what a feature needs, when you use it. You can refuse or withdraw any of them in your phone's settings; only that feature stops working.

  • Music and audio / files: to find and play your songs and, when you ask, to edit tags, rename, move or delete files. On Android you choose which folders are scanned.
  • Microphone: only for Listen (song recognition), and only while it listens.
  • Photos: none. When you pick a picture yourself (for example a cover or a Status video background), your phone's own picker gives NX Player only that picture.
  • Notifications: playback controls, the alarm, and news or offers if you allow them.
  • Local network (iPhone): for Wi-Fi transfer, phone sync, party mode and casting.
  • Tracking (iPhone): only for personalised ads, as explained above.

8. Legal bases

Where data-protection law requires a legal basis (for example the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023, the EU and UK GDPR), we and our service providers rely on:

  • Consent: personalised ads, tracking on iPhone, notifications and microphone access. You can withdraw consent at any time; this doesn't affect what happened before.
  • Contract: providing the app and NX Player Pro when you buy it.
  • Legitimate interests: showing non-personalised ads to keep the free version free, preventing ad fraud, keeping the app working and secure, and changing app settings remotely. We have balanced these against your rights; you can object (section 11).
  • Legal obligation: keeping purchase records where tax and consumer law require it. The stores and RevenueCat do this.

9. How long information is kept

  • On your device: until you delete it or uninstall NX Player.
  • iCloud sync: in your iCloud account until you turn it off and remove it in iCloud settings.
  • Services in section 4: for the periods in their own policies. For example, Google keeps advertising data for limited periods and anonymises it, and Firebase deletes installation data when it is no longer used.
  • Purchase records: as long as needed to provide Pro and as tax law requires.
  • Emails to us: until your question is resolved, then for at most 12 months.

10. International transfers

The services in section 4 operate in the United States and other countries. Information sent to them may be processed outside your country, including outside Nigeria, the European Economic Area and the UK. These companies protect such transfers with the safeguards the law requires, such as Standard Contractual Clauses or adequacy decisions, as described in their policies.

11. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you have the right to:

  • know what personal information is held about you and get a copy;
  • have it corrected or deleted;
  • restrict or object to its use, including for advertising;
  • receive it in a portable format;
  • withdraw consent at any time;
  • complain to a data-protection authority (section 12).

Most of your information is only on your device, and you control it there. We don't hold information that identifies you, apart from emails you send us. For information held by a service in section 4, you can use that company's tools, or write to us and we'll help you make the request. Email nextcraft.support@gmail.com. We answer within 30 days and never charge for it.

12. Nigeria, Europe, the UK and the United States

  • Nigeria: NextCraft follows the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023. You may complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.
  • European Economic Area and Switzerland: you may complain to the data-protection authority where you live or work.
  • United Kingdom: you may complain to the Information Commissioner's Office.
  • California and other US states: we don't sell personal information for money. Ads that use the advertising ID may count as "sharing" for cross-context behavioural advertising. You can opt out through your device's ad settings (section 6) or by using Pro. In the past 12 months the categories involved were identifiers (advertising ID, installation ID), internet activity (ad interactions), approximate location and commercial information (purchases). We don't knowingly sell or share the information of anyone under 16.

13. Children

NX Player is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has given personal information through NX Player, contact us and we'll help remove it.

14. Security

Everything NX Player sends over the internet uses encrypted connections (HTTPS). Information on your device is protected by your phone's own security. You can also lock NX Player with Face ID, a fingerprint or your passcode. No system is perfectly secure, but we keep what leaves your phone to a minimum.

15. Changes to this policy

If NX Player changes how it handles information, we'll update this page and the date at the top. For important changes, we may also tell you in the app.
