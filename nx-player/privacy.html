NX Player

Privacy Policy

NX Player by NextCraft Technologies · Last updated November 2, 2026

NX Player plays the music files you already have on your phone. We built it so your music stays yours: there is no account, and we never upload your songs or files.

What stays on your phone

  • Your music files, folders, playlists, likes, play counts, sound settings and backups stay on your device.
  • If you switch on iCloud sync (iPhone), your likes, plays and playlists are kept in your own iCloud account by Apple. We can't see them.
  • If you use Wi-Fi transfer or phone sync, files go directly between your own devices on your network.

What is sent to other services, and why

  • Song recognition (Listen, naming songs in mixes): a few seconds of audio fingerprint are sent to Apple's ShazamKit to find the song. No recording is kept by us.
  • Lyrics: the song title and artist are sent to LRCLIB to find lyrics.
  • Song info and covers: the song title and artist are sent to MusicBrainz and Apple's iTunes Search to find album covers and credits.
  • Radio: the station list comes from Radio Browser; streams come directly from each station.
  • Purchases (NX Player Pro): handled by the App Store or Google Play. We use RevenueCat to check whether you have Pro; it receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase receipts. We never see your payment details.
  • Ads (free version): Google AdMob shows ads. AdMob may use your device's advertising ID, IP address and general location to show and measure ads. On iPhone you choose whether apps may track you; in Europe and the UK you are asked for consent first, and you can change it any time in Settings › Ad privacy choices. Pro has no ads. See how Google uses data in ads.
  • App settings and notifications: Google Firebase Remote Config lets us change settings (for example, sales) without an update, and Firebase Cloud Messaging delivers news and offers if you allow notifications. Firebase uses an installation ID and a notification token. See Firebase privacy.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your data.
  • We don't ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • We don't upload your music, photos or files.

Permissions

  • Music / files: to find and play your songs.
  • Microphone: only when you use Listen, karaoke or recording, and only while you use them.
  • Photos: only when you pick a picture yourself (for example, for a Status video).
  • Notifications: playback controls, the alarm, and news or offers if you allow them.

Children

NX Player is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data.

Deleting your data

Everything NX Player keeps is on your device: deleting the app deletes it. iCloud data can be removed in your iPhone's iCloud settings. To ask about any data held by the services above, contact us and we'll help.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
