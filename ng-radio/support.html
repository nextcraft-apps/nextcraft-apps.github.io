Help & contact

NG Radio Stations by NextCraft Technologies

Need help, found a bug, or a station stopped working? Write to nextcraft.support@gmail.com. Tell us the station name and your phone model; a screenshot helps.

A station doesn't play

Some stations go off air or change their stream from time to time. Check your internet connection, then try another station. If one stays silent, tell us and we'll fix or replace it.

Favourites

Tap the ♡ on any station to save it; your favourites are under the red heart at the top of the Home screen. They are kept on your phone, also when you update the app.

Listening in the background

The radio keeps playing when you leave the app or lock your phone. Use the notification or the lock screen to pause or stop it. On Android you can also play stations from Android Auto in your car.

Ads

The app is free and shows a few ads. In Europe and the UK you are asked for ad consent when you first open the app.
