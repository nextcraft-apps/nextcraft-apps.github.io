Privacy Policy

NG Radio Stations by NextCraft Technologies · Last updated November 4, 2026

NG Radio Stations plays live Nigerian radio. There is no account, and we don't ask for your name, email or phone number.

What stays on your phone

  • Your favourite stations and app settings are saved on your device only.

What is sent to other services, and why

  • Radio streams: when you play a station, your phone connects directly to that station's stream (or to the service that carries it, such as Radio Garden, BBC, VOA or RFI). Like any website, the stream server sees your IP address.
  • Search: what you type in Search is sent to Radio Browser to find more Nigerian stations.
  • Ads: the app is free and shows ads from Google AdMob. We may also use AppLovin, Unity Ads or Start.io instead. These companies may use your device's advertising ID, IP address and general location to show and measure ads. On iPhone you choose whether apps may track you; in Europe and the UK you are asked for consent first. See how Google uses data in ads.
  • App settings and notifications: Google Firebase Remote Config lets us change settings (for example, which ads show) without an update, and Firebase Cloud Messaging delivers news if you allow notifications. Firebase uses an installation ID and a notification token. See Firebase privacy.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your data.
  • We don't record what you listen to on our own servers. We have none.
  • We don't ask for your contacts, photos, files or location permission.

Permissions

  • Internet: to play the radio.
  • Notifications: the playback controls, and news if you allow them.
  • Background audio: so the radio keeps playing when you leave the app or lock your phone.

Children

NG Radio Stations is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data.

Deleting your data

Everything the app keeps is on your device: deleting the app deletes it. To ask about any data held by the services above, contact us and we'll help.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
